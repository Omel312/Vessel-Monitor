How to read this. Each service shows two boards: Asia → US and US → Asia, built from the last regional port 7 weeks ahead, so vessels already working the region appear first (furthest advanced at the top). Each card shows vessel, voyage, IMO and capacity (TEU). Click a sailing for its full port-by-port route with ETA / ETD and the CY cut-off at each load port (N/A where MSC publishes none). Positions and last AIS come from VesselFinder (ocean-basin precision). Roster is rebuilt from MSC's live schedule. PF = proforma call date per the fixed-day weekly line schedule: the purple chip under each ETA is the proforma date, the badge is actual vs proforma in days.
